About this site

Written by someone who came from away

CFA: Come From Away. It's what Newfoundlanders call everyone else, with affection and mild suspicion in equal measure. This site is written from that position — close enough to know, far enough to notice.

No sponsorships. No tourism board partnerships. Just what it's actually like to live here.
