What you need to know

"The official guides tell you what the province wants you to hear. This one tells you what you'll actually find."

For international students, new immigrants, and anyone moving from away — the gap between brochure Newfoundland and real Newfoundland is significant. The fog is thicker. The winters are longer. The people are stranger, and warmer, than you expect. This site exists in that gap.

About this site

Written by someone who came from away

CFA: Come From Away. It's what Newfoundlanders call everyone else, with affection and mild suspicion in equal measure. This site is written from that position — close enough to know, far enough to notice.

No sponsorships. No tourism board partnerships. Just what it's actually like to live here.
